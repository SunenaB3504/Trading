## Executive Summary & Scope

**Mundane Astrology (*Medhini Jyothisham*)** is the branch of financial astrology that analyzes macro-level phenomena—including international conflicts, military escalations, trade embargos, supply-chain chokepoints, currency shifts, and nation-state policy changes.

Unlike company-level natal astrology (*Jathakam*), Mundane analysis evaluates global planetary configurations:
1. **Outer-Planet Hard Aspects:** Conjunctions, squares, and oppositions involving **Mars (*Mangal*)**, **Saturn (*Shani*)**, **Rahu**, and **Ketu**.
2. **Eclipses (*Grahana*):** Solar and lunar eclipse points falling on national or strategic zodiac signs.
3. **Solar Ingresses (*Sankranti*):** Annual solar entries into cardinal signs (*Mesh / Aries* and *Makara / Capricorn*).
4. **Sarvatobhadra Chakra (SBC) Aspect Rays (*Papa Vedha*):** Transit aspect rays cutting across phonetic coordinates of sovereign nations, commodities, and key corporate tickers.

## 🧪 Medhini / Mundane Quantitative Rules

Unlike natal company backtests, Track 3 tests **event-driven macroeconomic signals**:

### Rule M1: Mars-Saturn Conflict Conjunctions / Squares
* **Condition:** Transiting Mars comes into conjunction ($0^\circ$), square ($90^\circ$), or opposition ($180^\circ$) with Saturn within a $3^\circ$ orb.
* **Target Metric:** Forward 30-Day Percentage Return ($R_{30}$) for Defense & Metals vs. Civilian Consumer benchmark.
* **Hypothesis:** Defense and strategic metal stocks experience statistically significant upward momentum during geopolitical friction windows.

### Rule M2: Eclipse Points (*Grahana*) on Strategic Zodiac Signs
* **Condition:** Solar or Lunar Eclipse occurring in Aries (*Mesha*), Scorpio (*Vrischika*), or Capricorn (*Makara*).
* **Target Metric:** Forward 10-Day High-Low Range Expansion ($\text{DailyRange}_{10}$) and Maximum Drawdown ($MDD_{10}$).
* **Hypothesis:** Shipping freight and oil refinery stocks experience elevated price volatility and range expansion following eclipses in strategic signs.

### Rule M3: Major Solar Ingress (*Mesh / Makara Sankranti*)
* **Condition:** The Sun enters Aries ($0^\circ$ Sidereal — *Mesh Sankranti*) or Capricorn ($270^\circ$ Sidereal — *Makara Sankranti*).
* **Target Metric:** 30-Day Order Win Momentum ($R_{30}$).
* **Hypothesis:** Government defense contract allocations and defense stock momentum cluster in the 30 days following major solar ingress points.

### Rule M4: Sarvatobhadra Geopolitical Aspect Rays (SBC Vedha)
* **Condition:** Transiting Rahu or Saturn casting *Papa Vedha* aspect rays over national or sector phonetic coordinates on the 81-grid SBC.
* **Target Metric:** Forward 15-Day Maximum Drawdown ($MDD_{15}$).
* **Hypothesis:** Active SBC malefic rays over shipping or energy tickers flag supply-chain disruptions and export restrictions.
