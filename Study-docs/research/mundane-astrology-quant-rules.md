## Detailed Sub-Sector Analysis & Geopolitical Universe

### 1. Defense, Aerospace & Munitions
* **Core Universe**: HAL, BEL, BDL, Mazagon Dock, Solar Industries, Lockheed Martin, General Dynamics, Rheinmetall.
* **Astrological Drivers**: Mars (*Angaraka* - weapons, fire, engineering), Saturn (*Sani* - defense infrastructure, heavy machinery), and Rohini Nakshatra transits (*Rohini Shakata Bheda*).
* **Market Mechanics**: Transition from peacetime buffer maintenance to active military procurement. Mars-Saturn conjunctions trigger urgent defense capital outlays, accelerating order backlog expansion and margin expansion.

### 2. Crude Oil, Refineries & Maritime Shipping
* **Core Universe**: ONGC, BPCL, GE Shipping, CONCOR, Adani Ports, Frontline, Maersk.
* **Astrological Drivers**: Watery signs (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces), Moon/Venus (maritime liquidity/maritime routes), Saturn/Rahu (blockades, chokepoints, logistical friction), and Eclipses (*Grahana* in Koorma Chakra).
* **Market Mechanics**: Chokepoint blockades (Hormuz, Bab-el-Mandeb, Malacca) and maritime disruption drive immediate spikes in tanker freight rates (Baltic Clean/Dirty Tanker Indexes) and crude spot price volatility.

### 3. Strategic Metals, Critical Alloys & Minerals
* **Core Universe**: Midhani, Hindustan Copper, NALCO, NMDC, Vedanta, Freeport-McMoRan.
* **Astrological Drivers**: Mars (copper, iron, steel, armor-plating), Saturn (mining, minerals, heavy ores), and *Graha Yuddha* (Planetary War).
* **Market Mechanics**: Supply-side constraints driven by geopolitical export bans or mining labor disruptions coincide with acute demand spikes for defense-grade titanium, copper, and aluminum alloys.

### 4. Semiconductors, Defense Electronics & Tech Infrastructure
* **Core Universe**: Tejas Networks, CG Power, Kaynes Tech, Data Patterns, Astra Microwave, TSMC, ASML.
* **Astrological Drivers**: Mercury (*Budha* - silicon, data networks, microchips, communication hardware), Rahu (cyber warfare, electromagnetic interference), and 3rd/6th house Kota Chakra *Vedha*.
* **Market Mechanics**: Technological export bans, supply chain bottlenecks, radar hardware lead-time extensions, and cyber-defense capital expenditure cycles.

## Key Timing Windows & Quantitative Testing Guidelines

1. **30-to-60 Day Post-Conjunction Window**:
   - Hard transits (e.g., Mars-Saturn conjunctions or Rahu-Saturn alignments in Rohini) exert maximum impact within a 30 to 60-day window from exact orb alignment (<3°).
2. **90-Day Eclipse Shadow Window**:
   - Double Eclipses falling within 14 days create a geopolitical "shadow window" lasting up to 90 days, during which supply-chain vulnerabilities and maritime freight disruptions manifest.
3. **90-Day Quarterly Ingress Cycle**:
   - The annual *Mesh Sankranti* (Aries Solar Ingress) defines quarterly macro trends for state defense allocations, energy price trajectories, and commodity inflation.
