# Glossary

| Term | Plain meaning | Lessons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AMFI | Association of Mutual Funds in India; publishes the size-class list | equity-shares, mutual-funds-and-etfs |
| Anchor investor | A large institution given IPO shares before the public | ipos-rights-and-other |
| Bond | A loan you give to a borrower for interest | bonds-and-debt-funds |
| Compounding | Returns earning further returns | why-invest |
| Coupon | The yearly interest on a bond | bonds-and-debt-funds |
| Credit rating | An agency's view of the chance of default | bonds-and-debt-funds |
| Demat account | Account holding shares electronically | setting-up-in-india |
| Dividend | Cash a company shares per share | equity-shares |
| Duration | A bond's sensitivity to interest-rate changes | bonds-and-debt-funds |
| ETF | A fund that trades on the exchange like a share | mutual-funds-and-etfs |
| Expense ratio | Yearly fund fee as a percentage | mutual-funds-and-etfs |
| InvIT | Trust owning infrastructure assets | gold-reits-and-invits |
| KYC | One-time identity verification | setting-up-in-india |
| Market capitalisation | Share price times shares outstanding | equity-shares |
| NAV | Price of one fund unit | mutual-funds-and-etfs |
| Nominee | Person who receives your holdings if you die | setting-up-in-india |
| REIT | Trust owning rented property | gold-reits-and-invits |
| Rights issue | Offer of new shares to existing holders | ipos-rights-and-other |
| SIP | Fixed regular investment into a fund | mutual-funds-and-etfs |
| T+1 | Settlement one working day after the trade | setting-up-in-india |
| Yield | Return at today's price | bonds-and-debt-funds |
| Ask / offer | The lowest price a seller will accept now | order-book-liquidity-circuits |
| Bid | The highest price a buyer will pay now | order-book-liquidity-circuits |
| BTST | Buy today, sell tomorrow before shares arrive | delivery-intraday-margin |
| Circuit | The daily price limit for a stock | order-book-liquidity-circuits |
| DP charge | Fee for moving shares out of your Demat account on a sale | costs-of-trading |
| GTT | A trigger order valid for up to a year | order-types |
| Intraday | Buying and selling within the same day | delivery-intraday-margin |
| Leverage | Controlling a position larger than the money you put in | delivery-intraday-margin |
| Limit order | An order at your price or better | order-types |
| Liquidity | How easily you trade without moving the price | order-book-liquidity-circuits |
| Margin | Money you must deposit to hold a leveraged position | delivery-intraday-margin |
| Market order | An order filled at the best available price now | order-types |
| MTF | A broker loan to buy more shares than you can pay for | delivery-intraday-margin |
| Slippage | The gap between expected and actual price | costs-of-trading |
| Spread | The gap between bid and ask | order-book-liquidity-circuits |
| Stamp duty | State tax on the buy side | costs-of-trading |
| Stop-loss | An order that triggers when the price reaches a level | order-types |
| STT | Securities transaction tax | costs-of-trading |
| Anchoring | Fixing on a past price as a reference | behavioural-traps |
| Asset allocation | The split of your money across asset types | habits-that-help |
| Concentration risk | Too much money in one place | types-of-risk |
| Diversification | Spreading money so one failure matters less | types-of-risk, habits-that-help |
| FOMO | Fear of missing out | behavioural-traps |
| Loss aversion | Feeling losses more than equal gains | behavioural-traps |
| Rebalancing | Restoring your target split | habits-that-help |
| Survivorship bias | Seeing only those who survived | types-of-risk |
