## Q1 | lessons: why-invest

A savings account pays 4% and prices rise 6%. What happens to your buying power?

### Answer

It shrinks by about 2% a year in real terms.

## Q2 | lessons: know-your-position

Why should money needed in two years not go into shares?

### Answer

A fall of 40% may take years to recover, and you would be forced to sell low.

## Q3 | lessons: setting-up-in-india

If your broker fails, who owns the shares in your Demat account?

### Answer

You do. Shares sit in the depository in your name.

## Q4 | lessons: equity-shares

A share costs 50 rupees and another costs 5,000. Which company is bigger?

### Answer

You cannot tell. Size is price times number of shares.

## Q5 | lessons: equity-shares

A 90% fall needs what rise to recover?

### Answer

900%. A fall of 90% leaves 10, and 10 must become 100.

## Q6 | lessons: bonds-and-debt-funds

When market interest rates rise, what happens to existing bond prices?

### Answer

They fall.

## Q7 | lessons: gold-reits-and-invits

Why is a high REIT yield not always good news?

### Answer

A falling unit price can make the yield look high; it can signal trouble.

## Q8 | lessons: ipos-rights-and-other

What does it mean if you ignore a rights issue?

### Answer

Your ownership share shrinks as new shares are issued to others.
