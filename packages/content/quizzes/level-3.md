## Q1 | lessons: charts-and-indicators

Closes of 100, 102, 104, 106 and 108. What is the 5-day moving average?

### Answer

104, which is 520 divided by 5.

## Q2 | lessons: charts-and-indicators

A stock's RSI is 78. Does that mean it will fall?

### Answer

No. Above 70 is only called overbought. A strong stock can stay there for weeks.

## Q3 | lessons: breakouts-and-stops

You buy at 106 with a 5% stop. At what close does the stop sell?

### Answer

At a close of 100.70 or lower (106 x 0.95). The sale happens at the next open, which can be lower.

## Q4 | lessons: breakouts-and-stops

A share bought at 106 rises to a highest close of 120. With an 8% trailing stop, what close sells it?

### Answer

110.40 or lower (120 x 0.92).

## Q5 | lessons: breakouts-and-stops

After a 20% fall, what rise do you need to get back to the old high?

### Answer

25%.

## Q6 | lessons: rules-and-overfitting

You try 20 settings and the best looks excellent on past prices. Why be careful?

### Answer

With many tries, something looks good by luck (about a 64% chance at a 5% luck level). Test it on prices you did not use to choose it, and count the charges.

## Q7 | lessons: rules-and-overfitting

What is look-ahead in a backtest?

### Answer

Letting the test use information that was not known at the time, such as later prices. Cutting off all prices after a date should not change what happened before it.

## Q8 | lessons: short-selling-and-entry-methods

Why can a short seller lose more than the starting amount?

### Answer

A price can rise without limit but can only fall to zero. Selling at 100 and buying back at 300 loses 200 a share.
