## Q1 | lessons: tax-for-traders

You hold a share for 8 months and sell it at a ₹50,000 gain. Short-term or long-term, and what is the tax before cess?

### Answer

Short-term, because it was held 12 months or less. Tax is 20% of 50,000 = ₹10,000, plus 4% cess.

## Q2 | lessons: tax-for-traders

What kind of income are futures and options profits, and how are they taxed?

### Answer

Non-speculative business income, taxed at your slab rate.

## Q3 | lessons: tax-for-traders

Which losses can a long-term capital loss be set off against?

### Answer

Only long-term gains. Unused capital losses carry forward for 8 years if the return is filed on time.

## Q4 | lessons: scams-and-false-promises

A group promises 5% a month with no risk. Why should you assume it is false?

### Answer

5% a month compounds to about 80% a year. A scheme that could do that reliably would not need your deposit, and a guaranteed return is a warning sign in itself.

## Q5 | lessons: scams-and-false-promises

You think you have been cheated by a tips group. List the first steps.

### Answer

Stop sending money, keep the messages and transfers, report to the cyber crime helpline 1930 and your bank, complain on SEBI SCORES if a registered entity is involved, and tell the police. Do not pay anyone who offers to recover the money for a fee.

## Q6 | lessons: crashes-in-context

How far did the Nifty fall in 2008 and in 2020, and which took longer to recover?

### Answer

About 59.9% in 2008 and 37.7% in 2020. The 2008 fall took nearly three years to regain the old peak; 2020 took under ten months.

## Q7 | lessons: investor-protection

Where are your shares held, and what does the protection fund not cover?

### Answer

In your name at the depository, not with the broker. Protection funds give limited compensation if a member defaults. They do not cover losses from market falls or your own choices.
