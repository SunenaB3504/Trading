## Q1 | lessons: derivatives-basics

A Nifty lot is 65 units and the index is 22,600. What is the contract value, and what does that tell you about a 7,800 rupee option premium?

### Answer

22,600 x 65 = 14,69,000 rupees. A premium of 7,800 controls a contract about 188 times larger, which is the leverage.

## Q2 | lessons: futures-margin-mtm

You buy one Nifty future at 22,600, lot 65. The next day it settles at 22,450. What happens to your account that evening?

### Answer

You are debited (22,600 - 22,450) x 65 = 9,750 rupees in cash. This daily settlement is mark-to-market. If your balance falls below the margin needed, you must add money or the position is closed.

## Q3 | lessons: options-and-payoffs

You buy a call with strike 22,700 for 120. What is the break-even at expiry, and how much can you lose?

### Answer

Break-even is 22,700 + 120 = 22,820. The most you can lose is the premium, 120 a unit (7,800 on a lot of 65).

## Q4 | lessons: options-and-payoffs

Which has a known maximum loss: a short straddle or an iron condor, and why?

### Answer

The iron condor. Its bought outer options cap the loss. A short straddle has no bought protection, so its loss is not limited.

## Q5 | lessons: option-price-and-greeks

A call costs 290 with the index 200 above its strike. How much is intrinsic value and how much is time value?

### Answer

Intrinsic value is 200 (index minus strike). Time value is the other 90.

## Q6 | lessons: option-price-and-greeks

You buy a call, the index rises, and the option still loses value. Name two reasons.

### Answer

Time passing (theta) and a fall in implied volatility (vega) can take away more than the rise in the index (delta) adds. The rise may also have been too small.

## Q7 | lessons: expiry-and-settlement

You hold an index option that is slightly in the money at the close on expiry day. What happens, and how does it differ from a stock option?

### Answer

It is exercised automatically and settled in cash for its intrinsic value, with tax on that value. A stock option settles by delivery of the shares, so you would need the full value of the shares.

## Q8 | lessons: leverage-costs-and-tax

You lose 3 lakh rupees in F&O in a year and have a 12 lakh rupee salary. Does the loss reduce your salary tax?

### Answer

Not in general. F&O is business income, and a business loss usually cannot be set off against salary. It can be carried forward for up to 8 years against later business income if you file on time.
