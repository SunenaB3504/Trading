## Q1 | lessons: order-types, order-book-liquidity-circuits

A stock has a last price of 100, a best offer of 100.50 for 50 shares, and you place a market order for 500. What risk do you face?

### Answer

The order fills at rising prices; your average price could be well above 100.50.

## Q2 | lessons: order-types

You set a stop-loss at 180 and the stock opens at 150. Where does it fill?

### Answer

Near 150, the opening price, not at 180.

## Q3 | lessons: delivery-intraday-margin

With 5x leverage, how much does a 4% fall cost you, as a share of your money?

### Answer

20% of your money.

## Q4 | lessons: costs-of-trading

A trade has a round-trip cost of 0.3%. How much of the capital goes to costs after 100 such trades?

### Answer

About 30%.

## Q5 | lessons: types-of-risk

In this project's data, what share of eligible NSE stocks fell 70% or more and never recovered half?

### Answer

1,022 of 3,616, about 28%.

## Q6 | lessons: habits-that-help

You hold 60/40 shares and bonds. Shares rise and you now hold 66/34. What do you do?

### Answer

Sell some shares and buy bonds to restore 60/40.
