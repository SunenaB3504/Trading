## 🧪 5. Brihat Parasara Hora Shastra (BPHS) Testing Specifications

### Rule B1: BPHS Pancha-Tattva (Elemental) Alignment Rule
* **Astrological Logic:** Evaluates whether transits of elemental lords through corresponding zodiac signs (e.g., Venus/Moon in Water signs Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces for Agrochemicals/Sugar) generate 20-day excess returns compared to non-aligned transits.

### Rule B2: BPHS Vimshottari Dasha & Dhana Yoga Rule
* **Astrological Logic:** Tests whether major stock rallies coincide with active *Dhana-Yoga Karaka* Dasha/Bhukti periods, and whether seasonal commodity drawdowns are amplified when functional 8th/12th house malefic Dasha lords are active.

### Rule B3: BPHS Graha Drishti Aspect Protection
* **Astrological Logic:** Tests whether full BPHS benefic aspects (*Purna Drishti*) cast on the company's natal 2nd House Lord (Treasury) or Indu Lagna buffer the stock against broader macro commodity market crashes.

### Rule B4: BPHS Samudaya Ashtakavarga (SAV) Resilience
* **Astrological Logic:** Evaluates whether companies whose natal 2nd and 11th houses possess high Ashtakavarga scores (≥30 SAV bindus) demonstrate lower maximum drawdown depth during adverse weather events.
