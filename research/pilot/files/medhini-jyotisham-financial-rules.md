## Executive Summary & Methodological Framework

In classical **Medhini Jyotisham** (Mundane and Agrarian Astrology) as codified by Varahamihira in the *Brihat Samhita*, commodity pricing, agricultural yields, and raw material availability are governed by systemic interactions between planetary transits (*Graha-gocara*), seasonal solar ingresses (*Sankranti*), atmospheric pregnancy indicators (*Garbha-lakshana*), and elemental sound/directional vectors (*Tattvas* and *Vatachakra*).

Translating these classical principles into modern quantitative equity and commodity frameworks reveals a structured, time-bound predictive model:

1. **Supply-Side Drivers (*Sasyajātaka* & *Garbha-lakshana*):** Crop yields and hydro-reservoir levels are modeled using 195-day gestation cycles from cloud pregnancy detection to monsoon delivery. Bumper harvests (*Subha Vedha*) expand corporate operating margins for agro-input and consumer staple firms, while pestilence/drought (*Papa Vedha*) triggers supply-chain crises and margin compression.
2. **Price Fluctuation & Inventory Cycles (*Arghakaṇḍa*):** Systematic inventory accumulation rules based on solar ingress signs (*Rashi*) and planetary aspects define explicit 30-day, 60-day, 180-day, and 2-year holding periods for capturing commodity price surges and inventory revaluation.
3. **Sectoral Commodity-to-Planet Mappings (*Dravya-niścaya*):** Natural materials are categorized by planetary rulers:
   - **Venus:** Sugar, Cotton, Silks, Fragrant Oils.
   - **Mars:** Base & Precious Metals, Copper, Fire, Armaments.
   - **Saturn:** Petroleum, Coal, Iron Ore, Bitumen, Lead.
   - **Mercury:** Edible Oils, Ghee, Spices, Grains, Trade Merchandise.
   - **Moon:** Water Resources, Dairy, Liquids, Coastal Products.
   - **Sun:** Gold, Copper, Timber, Royal/State Commodities.
   - **Jupiter:** Top-Grade Foodgrains, Seeds, Yellow Produce, Agricultural Wealth.

## Focus Domain Syntheses & Testable Hypotheses

### 1. Agriculture & Agrochemicals
* **Monsoon Forecasting & Gestation:** Varahamihira establishes a strict 195-day gestation period between the initial conception of rain clouds (*Garbha*) during the dark fortnight of *Pushya* or *Margasira* and their actual precipitation during *Sravana/Bhadrapada*. A full 5-concomitant cloud pregnancy (wind, rain, lightning, thunder, cloud) yields a full *Drona* of rainfall per 100 Yojanas.
* **Pestilence vs. Productivity:** When the Sun enters winter signs (Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius) and is afflicted by Mars or Saturn at harvest, crop output suffers heavy pest damage, driving up chemical crop-protection demand and spot agricultural pricing.

### 2. Soft Commodities (Sugar, Oils, Cotton, Grains)
* **Sugar & Edible Oils:** Venus governs sugarcane, silk, and fragrant oils, while Mercury governs oilseeds, ghee, and trade merchandise. Solar ingress into Cancer (*Karkataka*) requires accumulating sugar and oil inventories and selling at the 60-day mark for maximum inventory revaluation. Mercury aspecting an eclipsed luminary triggers acute price spikes in edible oils and ghee.
* **Cotton & Grains:** Cotton falls under Gemini, Visakha, and Purvaphalguni. Storing cotton apparel and crops during the Libra (*Tula*) solar ingress yields 100% price gains when liquidated 180 days post-ingress.

### 3. Clean & Renewable Energy (Hydro, Solar, Wind)
* **Hydro Power & River Flows:** Initial precipitation in high-yield Nakshatras (e.g., *Rohini*, *Purvaphalguni*, *Uttarabhadra*, yielding 25 Dronas) forecasts peak seasonal water inflow into hydro reservoirs.
* **Wind Energy Cycles:** The *Vatachakra* (Wind Circle) rules that cool, scented north-easterly gales at the Ashadha Full Moon guarantee continuous rainfall, high river discharges, and sustained wind velocity for renewable generation.

### 4. Extractive Minerals & Mining
* **Metals, Iron & Coal:** Mars governs copper, gold, fire, and weapons; Saturn governs iron, lead, bitumen, and petroleum; the Sun governs gold and copper.
* **30-Day Mining Ingress Rule:** Solar ingress into Capricorn (*Makara*)—the sign governing iron and underground minerals—triggers a 30-day price rally in metallic vessels, raw iron ore, and mining equities. Malefic planetary conflicts (*Graha-yuddha*) involving Mars and Saturn in metallic signs generate structural supply deficits and metal market price surges.
