## 📋 Detailed Tier Specifications & Multi-Instrument Rules

### Tier 1: 🔴 RED — Structural Risk (4-House Screening Protocol)
* **Astrological Trigger:** Severe afflictions to the 5th (capital/loan book), 6th (debt liabilities), 8th (hidden NPAs/regulatory shocks), or 12th (capital leakage/liquidation) houses in the Company Jathakam or active Dasha period.
* **Fundamental State:** Structural insolvency, governance breakdown, or severe corporate distress (e.g., Aban Offshore, DHFL, Reliance Capital, Paytm).
* **Instrument Execution:**
  * **Cash Equities:** **HARD BUY BLOCK (0% Capital Allocation)**. Existing positions must be exited immediately.
  * **Long Futures / Call Options:** **PROHIBITED**. High risk of gap-down circuit locks and permanent equity destruction.
  * **Short Futures / Put Options:** **Permitted for Bearish Trades** (if liquid). Buying Out-of-the-Money (OTM) Put Options or Bear Put Spreads captures structural downside with capped risk.
  * **NCDs / Corporate Bonds:** **AVOID / DISINVEST**. High probability of rating downgrades or default.

---

### Tier 2: 🔴 RED — Active Transit Shock (PH-04 SBC Papa Vedha & Kota Breach)
* **Astrological Trigger:** Transiting Saturn, Rahu, or Sun projecting *Papa Vedha* rays on natal ticker coordinates, or transiting Mars crossing *Pravesha/Nirgama Nadis* into the *Kota Stambha* (fortress core).
* **Fundamental State:** Healthy company facing temporary acute price drag, panic selling, or negative news flow.
* **Instrument Execution:**
  * **Cash Equities:** **NO FRESH BUYS**. Flagged as `TRANSIT_WATCH_RED`. Existing holdings trigger trailing stop-losses or profit locks.
  * **Long Futures:** **PROHIBITED**. Sharp volatility can trigger premature stop-outs or margin calls.
  * **Hedging Options Strategies:**
    * **Protective Put Options:** Buy At-The-Money (ATM) Puts to lock in cash portfolio value.
    * **Covered Call Writing:** Sell OTM Call Options against long cash inventory to harvest premium during downside/sideways drag.
    * **Bear Put Spreads:** Capped-risk tactical short hedge.

### Tier 3: 🟡 YELLOW — Execution Guardrails (PH-03 & PH-09)
* **Astrological Trigger:**
  * *Stock Level (PH-03):* Transiting malefics in the 8th house from the stock's Natal Moon (drawdown drag).
  * *Trader Level (PH-09):* Transiting Moon in the 8th house from the **Trader's Natal Moon** (Personal Chandrashtama — emotional/cognitive execution bias).
* **Fundamental State:** Mild price drag on stock (PH-03) or personal execution vulnerability for the trader (PH-09).
* **Instrument Execution:**
  * **Cash Equities:** **50% Position Sizing**. Enforces **LIMIT Orders only** with strict price collars. Market orders, intra-day leverage (MIS/MTF), and impulse trading are blocked.
  * **Naked Options (Calls/Puts):** **PROHIBITED**. High theta decay and gamma risks during emotional mistiming lead to poor fills.
  * **Defined-Risk Spreads:** **Bull Put Spreads** or **Credit Collar Spreads** (collecting premium with defined max loss).
  * **Index ETFs:** During personal Chandrashtama days (PH-09), switch from individual stock-picking to **Broad Index ETFs (e.g., Nifty 50 ETF, Nifty Bank ETF)** to eliminate single-stock selection bias.

    * **Bull Call Spreads:** Buy ATM Call / Sell OTM Call to capture the recovery swing while keeping volatility costs low.
