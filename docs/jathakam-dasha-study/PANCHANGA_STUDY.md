## 1. Executive Summary & Key Findings

1. **Ambient Hostile Yogas (Vyatipata & Vaidhriti) Cause Massive Negative Alpha:**
   - Evaluated across **86,968 stock-days**:
   - While regular Yogas generate **+9.33%** annualized alpha, the two classical Mahapata Yogas (**#17 Vyatipata** and **#27 Vaidhriti**) plunge to **-9.58% negative alpha**!
   - This represents an annualized underperformance drag of **-18.91% per year**, with a surge in daily severe selloffs (>3% drop frequency = 10.47%).
2. **Chandrashtama Acts as a Deadly Accelerator under `[D-D-D]` (-24.99% Alpha):**
   - On its own, Chandrashtama causes a mild drag on healthy stocks.
   - **However, when an already vulnerable company in `[D-D-D]` experiences Chandrashtama (1,290 days), annualized alpha collapses to -24.99%!**
   - Chandrashtama acts as the final fatal catalyst that triggers panic liquidation in fundamentally deteriorating stocks.
3. **`[T-T-T]` Bullish Immunity:**
   - Under Triple Trikona (`[T-T-T]`), corporate fortune overpowers ambient Panchanga friction. Even on adverse Panchanga days, annualized alpha remains strong (+44.01% during Chandrashtama, +43.40% during Rikta Tithis). Corporate Dasha sets the secular trend, while Panchanga dictates tactical volatility.
