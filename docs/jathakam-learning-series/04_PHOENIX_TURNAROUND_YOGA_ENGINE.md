### 1.1 The Shastric Sutras (*Phaladeepika*, Chapter 6, Slokas 57–69)
Mantreshwara's *Phaladeepika* codifies the three distinct species of Vipareeta Raja Yoga:

$$\text{Harsha Yoga} \iff \text{Lord}(6) \in \{6\text{th}, 8\text{th}, 12\text{th} \text{ House}\}$$
> *"The native blessed with Harsha Yoga is endowed with boundless vigor, conquers all operational adversaries, commands unyielding physical and institutional vitality, and amasses wealth by overcoming financial crises."*

$$\text{Sarala Yoga} \iff \text{Lord}(8) \in \{6\text{th}, 8\text{th}, 12\text{th} \text{ House}\}$$
> *"The native possessing Sarala Yoga is fearless, long-lived, prospers from the sudden misfortunes or bankruptcies of peers, and turns unexpected crises into lasting financial dynasties."*

$$\text{Vimala Yoga} \iff \text{Lord}(12) \in \{6\text{th}, 8\text{th}, 12\text{th} \text{ House}\}$$
> *"The native endowed with Vimala Yoga is frugal, maintains strict control over capital expenditure, is universally respected for financial discipline, and amasses independent reserves."*

### 1.2 Neecha Bhanga Raja Yoga (NBRY) Rules
When a key corporate lord is in its sign of debilitation (*Neecha*), it generally indicates balance-sheet distress or depressed market valuation. However, under *Brihat Parasara Hora Shastra*, four canonical conditions trigger cancellation and convert the weakness into an explosive sovereign engine:
1. **Rule 1 (Dispositor Kendra):** The lord of the sign where the debilitated planet sits is in a Kendra (1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th house) from Lagna or Moon.
2. **Rule 2 (Exaltation Lord Kendra):** The planet that gets exalted in that same sign is in a Kendra from Lagna or Moon.
3. **Rule 3 (Direct Aspect):** The debilitated planet is directly aspected by its dispositor.
4. **Rule 4 (Navamsha Exaltation):** The debilitated planet occupies its sign of exaltation in the D-9 Navamsha chart.
